01 Why SQAAF

Quality cannot be assumed. It must be measured, witnessed, and improved.

For decades, school evaluations in India have been compliance-led rather than improvement-led. SQAAF reverses this — providing a single, structured framework that schools can use to honestly assess themselves and chart a forward path.

i.

Fragmented assessment standards across boards

State boards, CBSE, ICSE, and international boards have historically used incomparable quality benchmarks — making equity in school evaluation impossible.

ii.

Compliance over continuous improvement

Existing audits focus on minimum standards rather than evolving practice. SQAAF embeds a growth-oriented model into the assessment itself.

iii.

No path from finding to action

Without a structured framework, schools struggle to translate inspection findings into concrete next steps. SQAAF closes this loop with a 3-step process.

SQAAF responds to the call of NEP 2020 — establishing equitable, learner-centric, evidence-based quality assurance as a national standard.

03 Levels of Performance

Three tiers. One upward trajectory.

Every sub-domain question is rated against three benchmarks — drawn from the Sanskrit lexicon of educational growth — that capture not just where a school stands, but where it is headed.

LEVEL 01
Abhilasha
अभिलाषा · "Aspiration"
<60%
  • Minimal or inconsistent implementation of quality practices
  • Limited adherence to national and regional educational standards
  • Lack of structured systems for planning, monitoring, and feedback
  • Inadequate resources or infrastructure to meet benchmarks
LEVEL 02
Pragati
प्रगति · "Progress"
60-75%
  • Partial alignment with SQAAF standards
  • Moderate engagement of stakeholders
  • Practices are functional but lack consistency or scalability
  • Identifiable room for improvement in monitoring systems
LEVEL 03
Jagriti
जागृति · "Awakening"
>75%
  • Complete alignment with national and SQAAF benchmarks
  • Implementation of innovative, learner-centred practices
  • Strong systems for monitoring, feedback, and improvement
  • Focus on inclusiveness, sustainability, and holistic development

05 The 3-Step SQAAF Journey

Not just rating — a deliberate path to improvement.

SQAAF is designed to move schools beyond self-rating into reflection, planning, and demonstrable change. The process is the product.

01

Self-Reflect

Systematically assess current practices across the 5 domains using data, evidence, and stakeholder feedback.

02

Analyse Gaps

Critically review strengths and improvement areas through the auto-generated domain-wise gap report.

03

Plan Action

Formulate and implement targeted interventions tied to specific sub-domain weaknesses with a roadmap.

04

Measure Impact

Reassess outcomes in the next cycle to guide continuous, evidence-based institutional planning.
